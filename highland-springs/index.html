About This Home

Person-centered care, close to the city and the outdoors

Our home in Highland Springs, Virginia offers a comfortable, welcoming setting for individuals with special needs, just a short drive from Richmond while still feeling like a quiet residential neighborhood. Sponsored residential care here blends professional support with everyday comforts and a relaxed pace.

As a Sponsor, we follow a person-centered approach, working with our individual and their family to help build the life they want. The home is set up for outdoor living and cozy nights in alike — from grilling on the deck to movie nights streaming a favorite show.

"A comfortable place to call home, with room to relax outside and in."

A huge backyard, a covered patio and deck built for grilling, and a small fish pond make outdoor time easy. Inside, a finished basement rec room with a pool table gives everyone a place to unwind.
