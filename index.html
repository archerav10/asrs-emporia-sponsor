About This Home

Person-centered care, built around nature and family

Our home in Emporia, Virginia provides a quiet, safe, and highly accessible community tailored for individuals with special needs to live comfortably while staying deeply connected to family. Sponsored residential care here blends professional support with an enriching, small-town lifestyle.

As a Sponsor, we follow a person-centered approach, working with our individual and their family to help build the life they want. Our focus is on outdoor living — when the weather permits, we take full advantage of everything nature has to offer.

"Come join us in building a connection with our community and nature."

We have a charming backyard hobby farm where we raise chickens for fresh eggs, and our rural community offers movies, bowling, local festivals, fishing, and plenty of parks and playgrounds nearby.
